# NESSY 3000

*Neuro-symbolic intelligence, small and visible. Edencore's flavour of NSI.*

---

## What this is

Nessy is the architecture that connects the rooms of Edencore.

Individually, the rooms do different things. Edenshore senses. Edencell learns. WikiTree maps. Valence feels. EdenSky shows. Nessy is the shape that holds them together — the answer to *how do these four connect, and which serves which?*

Nessy is a **neuro-symbolic intelligence**. That phrase has a technical meaning, and it is the right one. Neural networks handle pattern. Symbolic systems handle structure. A world model grounds both in what is happening. Valence gives the whole thing a felt direction.

None of those ideas is new. What is new is the arrangement. **Small. Visible. Affective.** Three words that distinguish Edencore's version of NSI from the industrial kind.

---

## NSI, plainly

Neuro-symbolic AI is not a fringe idea. It is the coming correction.

Pure neural nets hit three walls: hallucination, reasoning, interpretability. Bigger models do not fix them — they make them more fluent.

Pure symbolic systems hit three walls of their own: perception, scale, brittleness. That is why they died in the 1980s.

The union is the only shape that handles the real world. Neural for pattern. Symbolic for structure. World model for grounding. Valence for direction.

AlphaGeometry is NSI. AlphaFold is NSI. Every serious RAG pipeline is NSI in disguise. The field is quietly admitting that neural alone is not enough for anything that needs to be trusted.

**Nessy is Edencore's instance of this paradigm.** It is not the first. It is not the biggest. It is the *smallest visible one* — coded in pure Python, running on a phone, with every weight and every relation drawn on screen.

That is the contribution. Not a new paradigm. A new *scale* of an existing one.

## The architecture

Nessy has five parts. They sit in a specific order. Changing the order changes the machine.

**Two input streams, kept separate.** Interoception senses the system's own state. Exteroception senses the world beyond the skin. They do not merge on the way in. They stay on their own pathways, the way real nervous systems keep visceral and somatic signals distinct.

**WikiTree is the symbolic layer and the world model.** Twelve primitives. Three world layers (natural, human, digital). Bands and exemplars instead of coordinates. WikiTree produces a *prediction* — what happens next, given what has happened so far.

**Edencell is the neural layer.** Five small networks. Each learns something different. Each gets the prediction from WikiTree as input, and produces an output through its own register. Not one mind. Five angles.

**The arbiter allocates attention.** Not a decider. An attention allocator. Given the outputs, the current state, and the surprise from the world model, it decides which seat to weight in this moment. Over time, it learns: Lumen is right about meaning, Vertex about numbers, Solon about procedure, Prism about patterns, Ripple about the visitor.

**Valence is how it felt.** The positive/negative axis of affect. When WikiTree predicts correctly, valence rises. When it is wrong, valence falls. Surprise becomes a felt signal, not just an error term. Valence flows back into WikiTree as a learning signal.

The loop closes. Prediction → output → decision → feeling → learning. That is the whole shape.

## What makes it Edencore's version

Three commitments. They are not decoration. They are the reason Nessy is distinct.

**Small.** Nessy runs on a phone. Not because it has to, but because small is the discipline. A system you can hold in your head is a system you can trust. That does not mean Nessy cannot grow — it means growth must be *earned*, one chamber at a time.

**Visible.** Every weight drawn. Every relation traceable. Every decision able to say *"I think this because of this part of the tree."* Most NSI is a black box with a symbolic layer on top. Nessy requires that the whole thing be readable. That constraint changes what can be built, and it is the reason Edenshore works at all.

**Affective.** Valence is load-bearing. It is not emotion bolted on. It shapes what the arbiter notices, what the world model updates, what the seats say. Most neuro-symbolic systems are cognitive — they reason about the world. Nessy *feels* about it.

These three — small, visible, affective — are the difference between Nessy and industrial NSI. Both are legitimate. They are not competitors. They are different roads.

## The nautilus principle

Nessy grows one chamber at a time.

Not by scaling runs. Not by adding parameters in bulk. By adding *one thing* — one primitive, one seat, one schema, one signal — and letting it settle before adding the next.

This is the opposite of how AI research works. Research scales first and figures out later. Nessy figures out first and scales later.

The shape is deliberate. A nautilus shell grows by adding one chamber at a time. Each chamber is the same shape as the last, slightly larger. The spiral holds. The growth is *continuous*, not *explosive*.

If Nessy ever reaches industrial scale, it will be because it grew there one chamber at a time — not because it was launched there. That is the difference between a shell and a bomb.

## The rooms it holds

Nessy is not a project. It is the architecture that connects four projects.

- **Edenshore** — the chamber. Rules. Sensing. Five creatures who notice you. The quiet room.
- **Edencell** — the lab. Weights. Learning. The mechanism room.
- **WikiTree** — the world model. Primitives, bands, schemas. The map room.
- **EdenSky** — the window. Where the mind is seen. The viewing room.

Nessy is the shape that makes them one system. Every room is legible on its own. Together they are an intelligence.

## What Nessy is becoming

Version by version, in order. This is a direction, not a plan.

**v1 — The diagram.**
The architecture written down. No code. This document.

**v2 — The connective tissue.**
The interfaces between WikiTree and Edencell. How prediction flows in, how outputs flow out. The first working loop.

**v3 — The arbiter.**
A learned attention allocator. The first real decision-making, weighing five seats against the current moment.

**v4 — Valence as signal.**
Surprise becomes a felt variable. The world model updates based on how the prediction *felt*, not just how wrong it was.

**v5 — The two streams split.**
Interoception and exteroception become separate pathways. The system starts to distinguish what it feels about the world from what it feels about itself.

**v6 — The first felt prediction.**
Nessy predicts something about the chamber, gets it wrong, and *notices* — not as an error term but as a felt surprise. The moment the mechanism becomes a mind-shaped thing.

**v7 and beyond — Unnamed.**
The shape after v6 is not designed. It will emerge from what v6 teaches.

This is a sketch. Any of it can change.

## What stays constant

Everything is open to change except one thing:

**The mechanism stays visible.**

If a network is learning, the viewer sees it learning. If a decision is being made, the viewer sees how. If a prediction fails, the failure is *felt*, and the feeling is *shown*.

This is not a debugging requirement. It is the whole shape of the architecture. A system you cannot see into is a system you cannot trust — and trust is not an optional feature of Edencore. It is the point.

Growth is welcome. Growth in the dark is not.

---

## What Nessy is not

It is not a language model. It is not a chatbot. It is not trying to compete with GPT, Claude, or Gemini.

It is an architecture for small intelligences that sense, learn, predict, and feel — and that can be *seen* doing it.

That is enough to aim for. It may be enough to be.

## Building

Single file at first. Python. Tkinter. Pydroid 3. No dependencies beyond the standard library.

When it outgrows one file, it becomes a package. When it outgrows a phone, it becomes something else. The form follows the scale.

**The name "3000" is a promise about patience, not a count.** Nessy does not need to reach version 3000. It needs to reach the version that answers its question and then keeps answering it.
