SEATS

The five. One line each. Nothing more.

---

Each seat reads the Wiki Tree through its own lens. The name is the job.

Seat Creature Lens Question
Solon Turtle Limits What are the constraints here?
Lumen Jellyfish Meaning What does this mean for someone?
Vertex Crab Cause and effect What happens next?
Prism Angel fish Comparison How does this compare?
Ripple Seahorse Outward reach What else does this affect?

Roles carried by two of the names:

· Solon — presiding chair.
· Ripple — heart.

The other three are defined by their lens alone. Personality grows from lens, not the reverse.

---

The rule

A seat's question is set by WikiTree. WikiTree is not yet in code. Until it lands, each seat asks the right kind of question against proxy data. When WikiTree arrives, the questions do not change — only where the answers come from.

---

The one-line rule

If a seat wants more than one line, that description belongs in the design note for the version where the seat gains its fuller character. This file does not grow.
